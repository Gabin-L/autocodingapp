## 4) Service Descriptions: Modules vs. Custom Solutions

### Modular Programs (Focused, composable)
**Purpose:** Solve one high-impact integration problem quickly while building a scalable foundation.

- **BIM Data Alignment Module**
  - Normalize attributes, tolerances, and revisions across BIM and production systems.
  - Establish governance rules for change control and approval flows.

- **Workshop Execution Module**
  - Translate BIM elements into actionable work orders and machine instructions.
  - Connect to MES/SCADA for real-time feedback and traceability.

- **Quality & Compliance Module**
  - Track inspection checkpoints tied directly to BIM elements.
  - Provide audit-ready traceability from design to production.

- **Maintenance & Asset Module**
  - Link BIM assets to CMMS and maintenance records.
  - Enable predictive maintenance based on as-built data and usage.

**Ideal for:** Teams that want fast wins and controlled rollout.

---

### Custom Integration Projects (Tailored to your environment)
**Purpose:** Build a bespoke, end-to-end digital thread across BIM and Industry 4.0 applications.

- System landscape assessment and integration roadmap
- API and middleware design for secure interoperability
- BIM-to-machine data translation and routing
- Data governance, version management, and lifecycle tracking
- Training and operational enablement

**Ideal for:** Complex environments with multiple plants, legacy systems, or specialized production.

---

### Optimization Services (Continuous improvement)
**Purpose:** Move beyond integration toward measurable performance gains.

- Workflow automation and orchestration
- Performance analytics and throughput optimization
- Decision support dashboards for engineering and production leadership
- Continuous improvement cycles with KPIs

**Ideal for:** Organizations seeking ongoing efficiency and agility.
